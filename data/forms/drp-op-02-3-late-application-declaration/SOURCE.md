# drp-op-02-3-late-application-declaration

- **Form code:** D.R.P/OP/02/3 (printed spelling; drp.gov.lk normal.php also writes "D.R.P/OP/02/03")
- **Name (en):** Statement made to the effect that the Identity Card has not been obtained within the relevant time period (late NIC application declaration)
- **Name (si):** නියමිත කාලය තුළ හැඳුනුම්පතක් ලබා නොගැනීම පිළිබඳ ප්‍රකාශය
- **Name (ta):** நிர்ணயிக்கப்பட்ட காலத்திற்குள் அடையாள அட்டை பெறாமை தொடர்பான வெளிப்படுத்தல்
- **Publisher:** Department for Registration of Persons (DRP)
- **Source URL:** https://drp.gov.lk/en/assets/downloads/D.R.POP023.pdf (listed on https://drp.gov.lk/en/downloads.php)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-24
- **File:** official.pdf, 1,296,494 bytes, PDF 1.5
- **Page count:** 3
- **Used by procedure:** nic-application (only when applying more than one year after turning 15; Rs 2,500 fine)
- **Verification:** supported. Sinhala/Tamil names are researcher translations.

## Observed structure / field labels (from verification)

- Three versions in one PDF:
  - General version (D.R.P/OP/02/3): signed by the applicant, attested by the Grama Niladhari, recommended by the Divisional Secretary with a fine amount, approved by the Commissioner General
  - School version (D.R.P/OP/02/3/ii): signed by the Principal
  - Estate version (D.R.P/OP/02/3/iii): signed by the Estate Superintendent / Manager
- Cites section 44(1)A of the Registration of Persons Act
- Individual field labels were not transcribed by research.
