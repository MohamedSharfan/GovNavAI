## Observed field labels (from research, verified against the scan)

1. Name of Applicant and Address
2. Full name of person respecting whose death application is made? / Sex
3. Cause of Death (If known) / No. of Copies required (form notes: maximum 3 copies per application)
4. Father's full name (If known)
5. Mother's full name (If known)
6. Date of Death / Place of occurrence (Hospital, House No. and Street, Town or Village or name of Estate)
7. Registrar's Division / Revenue District
8. No. and date of Registration Entry (If known) / Period of search desired, if any (form says maximum 3 months; RGD website fee text says up to 2 years)
9. Stamps supplied in payment of charges / Value of stamps affixed here
- Declaration authorising search; Date; Signature of Applicant
- Receipt slip: Application No./Date, Applicant's Name, Value of stamps received, Certificate will be posted in ... days (officer-only)
