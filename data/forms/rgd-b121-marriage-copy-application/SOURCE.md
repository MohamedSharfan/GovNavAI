## Observed field labels (from research, verified against the scan)

1. Name of Applicant and Address
2. Whether the application refers to a General or Kandyan marriage
3. No. of Copies required (form notes: maximum 3 copies per application)
4. Full name of male party
5. Full name of female party
6. If marriage was solemnized by a registrar: (1) His name (2) Situation of his principal office (3) Registration Division (4) Place where solemnized
7. If marriage was solemnized by a minister: name and situation of Church
8. District in which marriage was solemnized
9. Date of marriage (if exact date is known)
10. If exact date of marriage is not known, period of search desired (maximum 3 months)
- Stamps supplied in payment of charges / Value of stamps affixed here
- Declaration authorising search; Date; Signature of Applicant
- Receipt slip: Application No./Date, Applicant's Name, Value of stamps received, Certificate will be posted in ... days (officer-only)

## Notes

- Scanned form, no text layer. Printed stamp fees (Rs 25 / Rs 50 / Rs 100) are out of date; current fees are Rs 120 / Rs 250 (RGD website).
